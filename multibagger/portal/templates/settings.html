AI agents (extraction, analyst, red team) only spend on health-qualified watchlist stocks: Compounder-lane stocks that met at least this many rules, and Inflection-lane stocks, which pass their own health checks instead (no leverage or promoter failure, positive operating margin, pledge 20% or less, no open hard red flag). On a stock's page you can still run a stage for any stock.

When stocks are left after a run, the agent starts its next run straight away until every eligible stock is done (up to 30 runs in a row). The daily cap, the kill switch or Stop ends the chain. Each agent takes the best-qualified stocks first: the analyst and extraction take the highest research priority first (Pass 1 rules met, the Inflection lane, the numbers-only inflection score and positive filing triggers), and the red team takes falling holdings and then the highest scores. Stocks whose inputs haven't changed are skipped. Make sure the daily cap covers it (the analyst is about $0.33 a company on Opus).
